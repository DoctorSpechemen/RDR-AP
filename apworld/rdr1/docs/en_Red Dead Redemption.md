# Red Dead Redemption (PC)

## What does randomization do to this game?

Story missions play in the **normal vanilla order**: the game decides which mission comes next, nothing is locked, and
every story mission you finish sends a check. Your own items come from other players' worlds (and from your own checks).

**Dead Eye is off** until you receive your first **Progressive Dead Eye** (4 levels). Logic expects you to have more Dead Eye
as the story goes on (1 for Mexico, 2 for the end of Mexico, 3 for West Elizabeth, 4 for the last mission), but the game never
stops you from playing ahead.

## What is a location check?

Depending on your options: story missions, stranger missions (the check is the last part of each stranger's story),
gang member kills (every 5 kills per gang, up to 50: Walton's Gang, Banditos, Reyes' Rebels and others), hat tricks
(up to 15), clearing gang hideouts, minigame wins (poker and
blackjack hands, arm wrestling, five finger fillet, horseshoes, liar's dice, duels), and shop purchases: gear, maps and guns
from every general store, gunsmith and tailor (one check per item, buy it in any store that has it; horse deeds are blocked).

Not tracked in this beta: random encounters, fame/honor milestones, bounties, challenge ranks, treasure map treasure.

## What items can be sent to me?

Dead Eye upgrades, weapons (unless you use vanilla weapon pickups), outfits, Max Ammo, Dead Eye Refill, cash
($15 / $35 / $55), and traps: animal ambush, gang attack, lawmen posse, stampede, slow motion,
drunk, out of bullets (half your ammo comes back after 20 seconds), Herbert Moon (he whispers in your ear) and a rare
Dead Eye drain. Every trap shows a warning first and fires 3-5 seconds later.

Guns and gear you pick up or buy that Archipelago has not sent you are taken away again after 5 seconds ("That weapon
isn't yours to keep."). Thrown weapons, the knife and the lasso are never taken.

## Saves

Each multiworld keeps its own progress in `rdr1_ap_seeds\<seed>\` in the game folder. Start a new game save for each
multiworld: missions already finished in an old save cannot be found again.

## Weapons

- **Vanilla**: pick weapons up as normal.
- **Cattleman Start**: you start with only the Cattleman Revolver and every other weapon is sent to you.
- **Random Start**: you start with one random gun and every other weapon is sent to you.

## Goal

- **John Story**: finish all 57 John Marston story missions.
- **Full Story**: also finish the epilogue where Jack goes after Edgar Ross.

The Undead Nightmare DLC and horse purchases are not part of this randomizer. See README.md for the full feature list.
