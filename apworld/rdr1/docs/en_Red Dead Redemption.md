# Red Dead Redemption (PC)

## What does randomization do to this game?

Story missions play in the **normal vanilla order**: the game decides which mission comes next, nothing is locked, and
every story mission you finish sends a check. Your own items come from other players' worlds (and from your own checks).

**Dead Eye is off** until you receive your first **Progressive Dead Eye** (4 levels: Dead Eye, auto lock-on, target
painting, fast recovery). Logic expects more Dead Eye as the story goes on: 1 from the Irish's missions and for Mexico,
3 for Landon Ricketts' missions and everything after them (his first mission teaches target painting and can't be finished
without it, so it can't be started before level 3), and 4 for the last mission.

## What is a location check?

Depending on your options:

- story missions (57) and stranger missions (sent when each stranger's whole story ends);
- gang member kills (every 5 kills per gang, up to 50: Walton's Gang, Banditos, Reyes' Rebels and others);
- clearing gang hideouts, hat tricks (up to 15);
- minigame wins (poker and blackjack hands, arm wrestling, five finger fillet, horseshoes, liar's dice, duels);
- wanted-poster bounties, dead or alive, counted in the order you turn them in;
- fame ranks (Greenhorn to Legend) and honor ranks (good: Honest Joe, Peacemaker, Hero; or bad: Rustler, Road Agent,
  Desperado);
- shop purchases: gear, maps, guns and tailor items from every general store, gunsmith and tailor (one check per item,
  buy it in any store that has it; horse deeds are blocked).

Not tracked in this beta: random encounters, challenge ranks, treasure map treasure.

## What items can be sent to me?

Dead Eye upgrades, weapons (unless you use vanilla weapon pickups), outfits (13; the law uniforms are not
included, the game only lets Jack wear them), and filler: Max Ammo, Dead Eye Refill, cash
($15 / $35 / $55), Fame +50, Honor +50, and satchel consumables (Snake Oil, Tonic, Medicine, Moonshine, Chewing Tobacco).

Traps: animal ambush, gang attack, lawmen posse, stampede, slow motion, drunk, out of bullets (half your ammo comes back
after 20 seconds), lightning (three strikes close to you), a rare Dead Eye drain, and the duel trap (a gunman calls you
out; win and it counts as a duel win, lose and you die). Every trap shows a warning first and
fires 3-5 seconds later.

Guns and gear you pick up or buy that Archipelago has not sent you are taken away again ("That weapon isn't yours to
keep."); thrown weapons, the knife and the lasso are never taken. Outfits work the same way: only outfits Archipelago sent
you stay (John's own clothes and Gentleman's Attire are never taken).

## In game

Press **F9** for the Archipelago tally: checks done per category, items received, Dead Eye level, outfits, cash and traps.

## Saves

The client swaps in a separate save for each multiworld (your normal saves come back when you close the game and the
client). Each multiworld also keeps the mod's progress in `rdr1_ap_seeds\<seed>\` in the game folder.

## Weapons

- **Vanilla**: pick weapons up as normal.
- **Cattleman Start**: you start with only the Cattleman Revolver and every other weapon is sent to you.
- **Random Start**: you start with one random gun and every other weapon is sent to you.

## Goal

- **John Story**: finish all 57 John Marston story missions.
- **Full Story**: also finish the epilogue where Jack goes after Edgar Ross ("Remember My Family").

The Undead Nightmare DLC and horse purchases are not part of this randomizer. See README.md for the full feature list.
